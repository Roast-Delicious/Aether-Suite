AetherLedger_Data.lua and AetherLedger_Routes.lua contain data derived from
QuestieDB's WoW Forever data,
https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB
(data/Forever/foreverQuestDB.lua, foreverNpcDB.lua and foreverObjectDB.lua, plus
support/Forever/Zones; quests on Questie's quest blacklist are left out).
The Questie team allows copying QuestieDB content under the GPLv3 license, but notes
that some historic contributors have not formally agreed to it (see QuestieDB's
LICENSE.md).

This copy is for personal use. If you share AetherLedger publicly, keep this notice,
release it under the GPL, and read QuestieDB's license first.
