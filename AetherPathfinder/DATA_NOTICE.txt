AetherPathfinder_Data.lua contains NPC names, titles, service flags, factions and
map coordinates derived from QuestieDB's WoW Forever data:
  https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB
  (data/Forever/foreverNpcDB.lua and support/Forever/Zones/areaIdToUiMapId.lua)

The Questie team allows copying QuestieDB content under the GPLv3 license, but notes
that some historic contributors have not formally agreed to it (see QuestieDB's
LICENSE.md). This copy is for personal use. If you share AetherPathfinder publicly,
keep this notice, release it under GPLv3, and read QuestieDB's LICENSE.md first.
"Questie" is the Questie team's name and is not used to name this addon.
