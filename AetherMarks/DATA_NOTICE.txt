AetherMarks_Unique.lua is derived from QuestieDB's WoW Forever data,
https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB
(data/Forever/foreverQuestDB.lua, foreverItemDB.lua and foreverNpcDB.lua): the
named NPCs that are the only source of a quest item.
