AetherItemID_Data.lua contains data derived from two open-source projects:

Quests (which quests need an item, which quests an item starts, quest names):
  QuestieDB's WoW Forever data, https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB
  (data/Forever/foreverQuestDB.lua and data/Forever/foreverItemDB.lua)
  The Questie team allows copying QuestieDB content under the GPLv3 license, but notes
  that some historic contributors have not formally agreed to it (see QuestieDB's
  LICENSE.md).

Recipes (which recipes use an item as a reagent, and their skill levels):
  AtlasLootClassic, https://github.com/Hoizame/AtlasLootClassic
  (AtlasLootClassic/Data/Profession.lua, Classic table), GPLv2.

This copy is for personal use. If you share AetherItemID publicly, keep this notice,
release it under the GPL, and read both projects' licenses first.
